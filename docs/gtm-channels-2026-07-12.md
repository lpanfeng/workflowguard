Title: WorkflowGuard – The Governance Layer Your AI Agents Deserve

We built WorkflowGuard because we saw a pattern: every team deploying AI agents faces the same problem. Agents execute fast, but who approves their decisions? Who audits what they did?

Most solutions are either:
❌ Too complex (Temporal, LangGraph)
❌ Too expensive (AWS Bedrock, Microsoft Copilot)
❌ No audit trail at all (raw agent frameworks)

WorkflowGuard fills this gap: lightweight, open-source, independent deployment. Think of it as the "security gateway" for your AI agents.

What we have today:
✅ Agent execution monitoring + audit logs
✅ Human approval workflows (Feishu integration)
✅ Cron scheduling + retry mechanism
✅ CSV import/export templates
✅ Mobile-responsive dashboard
✅ i18n (zh/en)

We're building for teams that want to ship AI safely, not just ship AI fast.

Launching on PH soon. Stay tuned! 🚀
