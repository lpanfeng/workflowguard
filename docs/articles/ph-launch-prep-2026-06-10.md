## 二、发布文案草案

### Tagline (副标题)
> **AI agents that do the work, humans who make the calls.**
> (AI 负责执行，人类把控方向)

### 一句话描述
WorkflowGuard is a human-in-the-loop workflow platform that lets AI agents execute tasks autonomously — but routes every critical decision through an approval gate. No more AI hallucinations causing chaos. No more bottlenecks blocking progress.

### 详细描述 (用于 PH 页面)
**150字版本：**
Most teams are stuck between two bad options: let AI agents run wild (risky) or approve every single step (slow). WorkflowGuard introduces a third way — AI-first execution with human approval gates at decision points.

Create workflows in three templates (customer support, content publishing, data entry), configure approval chains, and let AI handle the heavy lifting. When an approval is needed, your team gets notified in Feishu, email, or webhook — approve or reject in one click.

Built for small teams who want AI autonomy without losing control.

**250字完整版：**
WorkflowGuard is a human-in-the-loop workflow platform that bridges the gap between AI autonomy and human oversight.

**The Problem:**
AI agents can do amazing things, but they also hallucinate, misfire, and make costly mistakes. On the flip side, requiring human approval for every step kills productivity. Teams need a middle ground.

**The Solution:**
WorkflowGuard lets AI execute tasks autonomously but routes every critical decision through approval gates. Think of it as an AI that does the heavy lifting — plus a human who makes the final call on what matters.

**Key Features:**
- 🧠 **AI-First Execution** — AI drafts responses, processes data, generates content automatically
- 🛑 **Approval Gates** — Route decisions to the right people via Feishu, email, or webhooks
- 📊 **Audit Trail** — Every AI action and human decision is logged, searchable, exportable
- 🔌 **Feishu Deep Integration** — Receive approval cards directly in Feishu chat
- 🎨 **3 Ready-Made Templates** — Customer service triage, content publishing, data entry
- 💰 **Free to start** — No credit card required

**Who it's for:**
Small teams (1-20 people) who want to leverage AI agents without losing human judgment.

### Maker First Comment

> Hey Product Hunt! 👋
>
> I'm Panfeng, the solo founder behind WorkflowGuard.
>
> Here's the honest story: I was working with a small team that tried to fully automate our customer support with AI. It worked great — until the AI approved a refund that should have been rejected. We lost $2,000 and a customer's trust in one afternoon.
>
> That's when I realized: **AI autonomy without human oversight is dangerous. But requiring approval for everything makes AI pointless.**
>
> WorkflowGuard is my solution to this middle-ground problem. AI agents execute tasks automatically — drafting responses, processing data, generating content — but every decision that matters routes through an approval gate. Your team gets notified in Feishu (or email, or webhook) and can approve/reject in one click.
>
> **What's inside:**
> - 3 ready-made workflow templates (customer service, content publishing, data entry)
> - AI execution engine with 3 confidence levels
> - Multi-level approval chains
> - Full audit trail for compliance
> - Feishu deep integration (approval cards in chat)
> - Webhook & email notifications
>
> **What's coming next:**
> - Custom workflow builder (visual drag & drop)
> - Support for Claude/GPT-4o model switching
> - Team collaboration features
>
> Would love your honest feedback — what's missing? What would make this useful for your team?
>
> P.S. If you want to see it in action, I've set up a demo workflow that auto-creates when you sign up. 🚀
