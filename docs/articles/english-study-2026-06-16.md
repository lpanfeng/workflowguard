## 二、长难句分析（5句）

**1.** "Organizations deploying high-risk AI systems must implement comprehensive governance frameworks that ensure accountability, transparency, and ongoing oversight throughout the system lifecycle."

- **结构**：主句（Organizations must implement frameworks）+ 定语从句（that ensure...）+ 介词短语（throughout the lifecycle）
- **要点**：high-risk AI → comprehensive governance → accountability/transparency/oversight → lifecycle

**2.** "Compliance with the EU AI Act requires not only technical measures such as risk assessment and bias mitigation, but also organizational changes including the appointment of a dedicated AI governance officer."

- **结构**：主语（Compliance）+ 谓语（requires）+ 宾语（not only...but also...）
- **要点**：合规 = 技术措施 + 组织变革

**3.** "The principle of meaningful human oversight mandates that automated decisions affecting individuals' fundamental rights must be subject to review by qualified personnel before final implementation."

- **结构**：主语（The principle）+ 谓语（mandates）+ that宾语从句
- **要点**：meaningful human oversight → qualified personnel review → before implementation

**4.** "Adherence to responsible AI principles is not merely a regulatory obligation but a strategic imperative that distinguishes market leaders from laggards in an increasingly competitive landscape."

- **结构**：主语（Adherence）+ 系动词（is）+ 表语（not merely...but...）+ 定语从句（that distinguishes...）
- **要点**：合规不仅是义务，更是战略优势

**5.** "Robust safeguard mechanisms, including real-time monitoring, automated alerting, and incident response protocols, must be embedded within the AI system architecture to prevent unauthorized or harmful actions."

- **结构**：主语（Mechanisms）+ 插入语（including...）+ 谓语（must be embedded）+ 目的状语（to prevent...）
- **要点**：safeguard = monitoring + alerting + response → embedded in architecture
