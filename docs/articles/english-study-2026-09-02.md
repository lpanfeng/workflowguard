## 地道表达（10句）

1. **"The narrative around X is shifting."** — X相关的叙事正在转变。
   - 例句：The narrative around AI agents is shifting from "replacement" to "collaboration".

2. **"X is becoming commoditized."** — X正在变得商品化。
   - 例句：LLM APIs are becoming commoditized, so differentiation will come from use cases.

3. **"A targeted approach beats a generic one."** — 针对性方案胜过通用方案。
   - 例句：For enterprise workflows, a targeted approach beats a generic one.

4. **"Platform dependency is a survival risk."** — 平台依赖是生存风险。
   - 例句：Relying solely on Vercel for your SaaS is a platform dependency risk.

5. **"Build for the long arc, not the short sprint."** — 为长期弧线而非短期冲刺而建。
   - 例句：Community building is about the long arc, not the short sprint.

6. **"Transparency is the new trust currency."** — 透明是新信任货币。
   - 例句：In the AI era, transparency is the new trust currency.

7. **"The bigger isn't always the better."** — 越大不一定越好。
   - 例句：The AnkiDroid case shows the bigger (Google Play) isn't always the better.

8. **"Community resilience comes from autonomy."** — 社区韧性来自自主权。
   - 例句：Open source community resilience comes from autonomy.

9. **"Differentiation through narrative, not just features."** — 通过叙事而非仅功能来差异化。
   - 例句：Claude Fable 5.1's differentiation comes through narrative, not just features.

10. **"Pivot based on signals, not noise."** — 基于信号而非噪音来调整方向。
    - 例句：Product strategy should pivot based on signals, not noise.
