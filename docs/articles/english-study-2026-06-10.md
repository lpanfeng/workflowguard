## 重点句式分析

### 1. "Launch is not the finish line — it's the starting line."

**解析：**
- 这是一个比喻句式（metaphor），用 race 的「终点线」和「起跑线」来对比两种思维模式
- "not A — it's B" 结构：不是A，而是B
- **应用：** 很多工程师把产品发布当作终点，实际上发布只是验证假设的开始
- **仿写：** "The MVP launch is not the destination — it's the first data point."

### 2. "The best way to get your first 100 users is to do things that don't scale."

**解析：**
- "The best way to X is to Y"：做X最好的方式是Y
- "things that don't scale"：不能规模化的事情（YC创始人Paul Graham的经典概念）
- **语境：** 意思是早期要手动拉用户、一对一沟通，而不是做自动化营销
- **仿写：** "The best way to validate your product is to talk to 10 strangers who might pay for it."

### 3. "You should launch so many times that it becomes boring."

**解析：**
- "so ... that ..." 结果状语从句：「如此...以至于...」
- "launch so many times"：发布很多次
- "becomes boring"：变得无聊（强调次数之多）
- **核心思想：** 把发布变成常规操作，而不是大事
- **仿写：** "You should iterate so quickly that shipping new features becomes a daily habit."

### 4. "Most startups die not because they can't build the product — they die because nobody knows about it."

**解析：**
- "not because A — they die because B"：不是因为A而死，而是因为B
- 强调对比：很多创始人专注于写代码，忽略了分发和推广
- **应用：** 对 WorkflowGuard 的启示：代码写完了不等于产品做完了，还要让人知道
- **仿写：** "Most side projects fail not because the code doesn't work — they fail because nobody discovers them."

### 5. "Your launch strategy should match your product stage — what works for pre-revenue won't work for Series A."

**解析：**
- "match your product stage"：匹配你产品的阶段
- "what works for X won't work for Y"：对X有效的方式不一定对Y有效
- **核心观点：** 发布策略要随产品阶段而调整，没有放之四海而皆准的方法
- **仿写：** "The marketing strategy that worked for a free tool won't work for a paid SaaS product."

## 英语写作练习 — WorkflowGuard Launch Plan

Below is a short paragraph in English describing my launch plan for WorkflowGuard:

> **WorkflowGuard is a human-in-the-loop workflow platform that helps teams collaborate with AI agents safely.**
>
> My launch strategy has three phases. First, I will launch on Product Hunt to get initial exposure and collect feedback from early adopters. The landing page and onboarding flow are already built.
>
> Second, I will write technical articles about the problem WorkflowGuard solves — why AI agents need approval gates, why human oversight matters in 2026, and how small teams can adopt human-in-the-loop workflows without hiring more people. Each article is a distribution channel.
>
> Third, I will talk to every person who shows interest. As Kat Mañalac says, "Do things that don't scale." If someone comments on my article, I'll reply within an hour. If someone signs up, I'll email them personally within a day. The first 100 users should feel like they're getting a personalized experience.
>
> The goal is not to make a splash. The goal is to find 10-20 teams who love the product and iterate based on their feedback. Launch, learn, improve, launch again. 🚀

---

### 生词检查（用英文解释）

1. **Human-in-the-loop** — A system design where humans are involved in the decision-making process of an AI system, rather than letting the AI run fully autonomously.
2. **Approval gate** — A checkpoint in a workflow where a human must approve or reject before the process continues.
3. **Oversight** — The act of supervising or monitoring something to ensure it's done correctly.
4. **Distribution channel** — A way to reach potential users, such as Product Hunt, technical blogs, or social media.
5. **Early adopter** — A person who uses a new product before most people, often providing feedback to help improve it.
