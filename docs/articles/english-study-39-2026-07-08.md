## 二、长难句分析（5句）

### 句1（来自GLM-5.2讨论）
> "What we're witnessing is not just a price drop but a fundamental restructuring of who gets to build intelligent systems."

**结构拆解**：
- 主语从句：What we're witnessing
- 谓语：is
- 表语：not just a price drop but a fundamental restructuring
- 定语从句：who gets to build intelligent systems
- **翻译**：我们见证的不仅仅是一个价格下降，而是谁能构建智能系统的根本性重构。

### 句2（来自98% isn't much）
> "The problem isn't that AI is bad at 98%, it's that nobody tells you when it's in the 2%."

**结构拆解**：
- 主句：The problem isn't that...
- 对比结构：it's that...
- **翻译**：问题不在于AI在98%的情况下表现不好，而在于没有人告诉你它在那2%的时候出了什么问题。
- **启示**：这正是WorkflowGuard的价值——审计日志就是为了抓住那2%的异常。

### 句3
> "As inference costs approach zero, the bottleneck shifts from compute to governance."

**结构拆解**：
- 状语从句：As inference costs approach zero
- 主句：the bottleneck shifts from compute to governance
- **翻译**：随着推理成本趋近于零，瓶颈从计算转向治理。
- **核心洞察**：这句话定义了WorkflowGuard的市场时机。

### 句4
> "The companies that win won't be those with the best models, but those with the best governance frameworks."

**结构拆解**：
- 主语：The companies that win
- 谓语：won't be
- 对比：those with the best models / those with the best governance frameworks
- **翻译**：获胜的公司不会是拥有最好模型的公司，而是拥有最好治理框架的公司。

### 句5
> "Open-source AI is democratizing intelligence, but democratization without accountability is democratizing risk."

**结构拆解**：
- 并列句：but
- 对比结构：democratizing intelligence / democratizing risk
- **翻译**：开源AI正在使智能民主化，但没有问责的民主化就是在使风险民主化。

## 三、英语感悟（200字）

Today's HN discussion reveals a profound shift in the AI narrative. For years, the dominant story was "bigger models = better." Now, with GLM-5.2 achieving 2626 tok/s/node on consumer hardware, the conversation has pivoted to **"cheaper models = more governance needed."** 

The phrase "the bottleneck shifts from compute to governance" is the thesis statement for my generation of AI companies. It reminds me of the principle from Mao's "On Contradiction": as conditions change, the principal contradiction changes. When AI was expensive, the principal contradiction was "capability vs cost." Now it's "scale vs governance."

For WorkflowGuard, this is validation. Our positioning as a governance layer—not a model provider—is exactly right for this moment.
