## 地道表达

1. **"Mogging"** — 碾压、完胜
   - "China absolutely mogging the likes of OpenAI and Anthropic"
   - 用法：形容在某个领域全面超越

2. **"Give up on that"** — 放弃某想法
   - "I'm sure they have nothing to rival this... and have already given up on that"
   - 语境：认为某事不可能实现

3. **"Solid"** — 可靠的、靠谱的
   - 常用于评价工具/产品："Solid move" / "Solid choice"

4. **"Meat and potatoes"** — 核心本质
   - 虽然今日未出现，但这是技术讨论中常用表达

5. **"Ballpark figure"** — 大致数字
   - 商业讨论中常见："Let's get a ballpark figure"

## 句式模仿

1. **观点表达**: "I was quite surprised that [X] had [Y] to [Z]."
   - "I was quite surprised that Zai had deep pockets to serve this free for a week."

2. **对比分析**: "It's not [A], it's [B]."
   - "It's not a solved problem because each iteration of technology doesn't just fix the mistakes of the past, it's an evolution."

3. **预测判断**: "I wouldn't be surprised if [X] ended up [Y]."
   - "I wouldn't be surprised if AWS ends up using DuckDB somehow in their AI agents."
