## 三、长难句分析（5句）

### 句子 1
> "CRISPR tech selectively shreds cancer cells, including 'undruggable' cancers, without harming healthy tissue."

**结构拆解：**
- 主语：CRISPR tech
- 谓语：selectively shreds
- 宾语：cancer cells
- 后置修饰：including 'undruggable' cancers（插入语，举例说明）
- 状语：without harming healthy tissue（方式状语）

**翻译：** CRISPR技术选择性摧毁癌细胞，包括"难以用药"的癌症，而不伤害健康组织。

### 句子 2
> "Malware developers added nuclear and biological weapons text to their spyware, escalating the social engineering tactics used by cybercriminals."

**结构拆解：**
- 主句：Malware developers added nuclear and biological weapons text to their spyware
- 现在分词短语作结果状语：escalating the social engineering tactics
- 过去分词短语作后置定语：used by cybercriminals（修饰tactics）

**翻译：** 恶意软件开发者在其间谍软件中添加了核武器和生物武器文本，升级了网络犯罪分子使用的社会工程学手段。

### 句子 3
> "Fable orchestrates and reviews code while Codex handles the building, reducing token consumption by 80%."

**结构拆解：**
- 主句：Fable orchestrates and reviews code（并列谓语）
- while引导的让步/对比从句：while Codex handles the building
- 现在分词短语作结果状语：reducing token consumption by 80%

**翻译：** Fable负责编排和审查代码，而Codex负责构建，使Token消耗减少了80%。

### 句子 4
> "This legislation would fundamentally change the U.S. Copyright Office, potentially reshaping how AI-generated content is protected."

**结构拆解：**
- 主句：This legislation would fundamentally change the U.S. Copyright Office
- 现在分词短语作补充说明：potentially reshaping how AI-generated content is protected
- 宾语从句：how AI-generated content is protected（作reshaping的宾语）

**翻译：** 这项立法将根本性地改变美国版权局，可能重塑AI生成内容的保护方式。

### 句子 5
> "The rise of autonomous AI agents demands a new paradigm of governance—one that balances innovation with accountability."

**结构拆解：**
- 主语：The rise of autonomous AI agents
- 谓语：demands
- 宾语：a new paradigm of governance
- 破折号后是同位语解释：one that balances innovation with accountability
- 定语从句：that balances innovation with accountability（修饰one）

**翻译：** 自主AI代理的兴起要求一种新的治理范式——一种在创新与问责之间取得平衡的范式。
